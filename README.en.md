# AirNoti

[Русский](README.md) · **English**

AirNoti makes your **Google Fitbit Air** vibrate briefly when a notification arrives on your Android phone. It works in the background with notifications from all apps, including system and service notifications. There is no need to select apps individually.

**[Download the app](https://github.com/RoyMatus/airnoti/releases)**

## Getting started

1. Pair your Fitbit Air with your phone through Google Health.
2. Install AirNoti and allow Bluetooth and notification access.
3. Turn on the main switch. If one Fitbit Air is paired, the app selects it automatically.

No account, ads or data sent to a server. Notification text is not stored.

This is an experimental version: some alerts may be missed. The app is not available for iPhone. AirNoti is not affiliated with Google or Fitbit.
